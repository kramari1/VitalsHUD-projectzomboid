title=Vitals HUD
description=Vitals HUD adds a compact, expandable survivor status display for Project Zomboid Build 42.
description=
description=The compact HUD shows Health, Endurance, Fatigue, Hunger and Thirst at a glance.
description=
description=Press Details to view additional information about your survivor's current condition.
description=
description=Active conditions can include Pain, Stress, Panic, Sickness, Food Sickness, Wetness, Discomfort, Unhappiness, Boredom, Intoxication and Poison.
description=
description=Secondary conditions are automatically hidden when their value is zero, keeping the interface clean and showing only information that currently matters.
description=
description=Body temperature is shown using a cold / normal / hot gauge based on your survivor's current core temperature and normal temperature set point.
description=
description=The HUD is positioned to avoid the watch and moodle areas, and automatically hides while hovering vanilla moodles so their tooltip text remains readable.
description=
description=Vitals HUD is display-only. It does not modify character stats or gameplay.
description=
description=Zombie infection status is intentionally NOT shown.
description=
description=The goal is to provide some of the continuous physical feedback a real person would naturally have, without exposing hidden game-state information.
